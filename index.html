How the work gets done

Five questions that keep the investigation grounded.

The Foundation Framework. Gather. Evaluate. Target.

These aren't steps to follow in a particular order. They're questions that guide the investigation, helping us understand what's happening across the business, challenge assumptions, and identify what deserves attention before recommending a solution.

  • 1. Set the destination

    What do you actually want this business to become? Every option is measured against your answer, not against somebody else’s definition of growth.

  • 2. Get your bearings

    What is objectively true about the business today? Not what you assume. Not what you hope. What the numbers, the workflows and the people actually show.

  • 3. Read the signals

    What stands out, what does not fit, what evidence is missing, and what is being assumed? This is where symptoms get separated from causes.

  • 4. Find what matters most

    What is most likely limiting the business right now, or about to? Sometimes it is one issue. Often it is several, connected. That is where the leverage sits.

  • 5. Chart the course

    What should you do next, what are the tradeoffs, and why? A short ordered list you can start on Monday.

  • There can be more than one way forward. The goal is to understand the tradeoffs and determine which route makes the most sense for your business.
